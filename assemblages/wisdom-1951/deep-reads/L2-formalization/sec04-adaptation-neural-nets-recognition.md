# Section 4: Adaptation, Neural Nets & Recognition

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), pp. 7–20]*

## 1. Dynamic Steady Motion vs. Static Equilibrium (§4.0)

Wisdom sharpens Ross Ashby's equilibrium terminology:
- **Static Equilibrium:** Mechanical rest where velocity $v = 0$ (e.g. a pendulum arrested by friction, or biological death). Passive resistance to displacement.
- **Steady Motion (Dynamic Steady State):** An operating regime with non-zero velocities and active metabolic fluxes (e.g. an upright spinning top, an incubator, a healthy organism).
- **Adaptation Defined:** A system actively maintaining steady motion within an invariant envelope of essential variables ($E \in [E_{\min}, E_{\max}]$) through perpetual, self-correcting error-damping.

## 2. Clinical Falsification: Hunting and Ataxia (§4.1)

Negative feedback theory yields a deductive, testable physiological prediction:
- **Hunting (Purpose Tremor):** If a negative feedback loop possesses excessive gain or phase delay commensurate with error scale, it overshoots and enters divergent or sustained oscillation. Verified clinically in cerebellar lesion patients who experience violent hand tremors only when executing purposeful, target-directed reaching.
- **Sensory Ataxia:** In tabes dorsalis, sensory feedback pathways in the spinal cord are severed. Open-loop movement collapses unless visual feedback consciously compensates.

## 3. Autonomic Reorganisation & The Homeostat (§4.2)

- **The Limit of Simple Feedback:** A fixed differential equation $\dot{x} = A x$ cannot adapt if environmental perturbations invert the signs of matrix $A$ (e.g. crossed aileron controls).
- **The Step-Function Break:** Ashby models adaptive reorganization by treating system "breakdown" as a unified dynamical event governed by step-functions.
- **The Uniselector Mechanism:** When essential variables $E$ breach threshold limits, uniselector relays trip, randomly switching potentiometer resistances until a new coupling topology yields eigenvalues with negative real parts ($\text{Re}(\lambda_i) < 0$).
- **The Cartesian Refutation:** Because the Homeostat searches phase space autonomously via uniselector breaks, it discovers stable configurations never foreseen or calculated by its designer, refuting Descartes’ principle of causal reality.

## 4. Electroneural Analogies & Scansion Invariance (§4.3–§4.4)

1. **Regenerative Loops (Lorente de Nó):** Neurons forming circular synapsing nets maintain memory as continuous circulation of discrete all-or-none action potentials over time, rather than static localized inscriptions.
2. **The Pitts-McCulloch Scanning Transform:** Solves the recognition of geometric universals (Gestalten) regardless of retinal scale or position. Periodic alpha-rhythm scansion sweeps the visual field, mapping two-dimensional spatial arrays into one-dimensional temporal trains of invariant pitch at varying tempi.
