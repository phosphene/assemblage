# Section 3: The Basic Cybernetic Hypothesis

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), pp. 6–8]*

## 1. The Twin Postulates

Wisdom isolates the hypothesis of cybernetics into two stacked claims:

1. **The Physiological Postulate:** "The basic hypothesis of cybernetics is that the chief mechanism of the central nervous system is one of negative feed-back."
2. **The Behavioral Postulate:** "Secondly, cybernetics makes the hypothesis that the negative feed-back mechanism explains 'purposive' and 'adaptive' behaviour."

**Logical structure:** (1) is a neurophysiological claim about mechanism; (2) is a semantic/behavioral claim about what that mechanism *explains*. The two are separable: (2) could hold in machines while (1) fails in brains, and vice versa. Wisdom keeps them apart — an early instance of the platform's constitutive/causal separation.

## 2. The Operational Definition

Wisdom supplies the era's cleanest definition:

> "a simple negative feed-back mechanism [is] a mechanism by which part of the input-energy of a machine is utilised at intervals to impose a check on the output-energy."

Note the terms: *part of* (derivative, not independent), *at intervals* (discrete or continuous sampling), *impose a check* (regulatory, not generative).

## 3. Teleology Without Vitalism

The philosophical payoff: "purposive" is *defined* in terms of negative feedback rather than treated as a primitive.

- **Northrop distinction (footnote 1, p. 6):** A machine may start toward a goal and, after deviation, make no attempt to pursue it — *purposive* but not *teleological*. To be *teleological* it must continue to seek its goal. The distinction is regulatory persistence, not initial direction.
- **Predictive behavior:** The anti-aircraft gun firing at where the aircraft *will be* is covered by the same mechanism — "just as a cat chasing a mouse extrapolates the mouse's path." Prediction is a property of properly damped feedback loops, not a separate faculty.

## 4. Attribution & Priority

Wisdom assigns the hypothesis "mainly to Ashby and Wiener": Ashby introduced it in a 1940 paper; Wiener put it forward independently in the 1943 joint paper (Rosenblueth-Wiener-Bigelow). It was Wiener who coined the word "cybernetics" in his book. This establishes the canonical priority lineage the platform's bibliographic apparatus tracks.

## 5. Platform Coordinates

- **Level 3 (epistemic-levels):** The twin postulates are first-order research program commitments — testable, falsifiable, held by specific programs.
- **Constitutive/causal:** "Purposive" is constituted by feedback topology (what it *is*); extrapolation and hunting are causal dynamics (what it *does*).
