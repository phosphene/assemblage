# Section 6: Electro-encephalography

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), pp. 20–22]*

## 1. The Epistemic Function of the EEG Section

Wisdom's treatment of EEG (Adrian's work on alpha rhythms) is not a report of findings but an argument about *what kind of support* electroneural evidence can provide. The pivotal passage:

> "What this result gives, therefore, is not striking information about electroneural nets but support for the hypothesis of electroneural nets."

**The carve:** The McCulloch-Pitts formalism requires that the brain's substrate possess the formal properties needed for mathematical and logical inference (closure under logical operations, temporal sequencing). Evidence that cortical rhythms have the *right formal properties* does not reveal new facts about the nets — it raises the *prior probability* of the net hypothesis itself. This is confirmation-structure reasoning, not discovery-structure.

## 2. Craik's Symbolic Models

Wisdom credits Kenneth Craik's *The Nature of Explanation* (1943): when contemplating purposive action, one considers a "symbolic model" of the action. Wisdom's assessment is measured:

> "Unfortunately his treatment was too sketchy to be illuminating. But he evidently entertained hope of being able to describe abstract conceptions, such as meaning, implication, number, and so on in terms of such models. There can be little doubt that all this is true in some sense or other; but whether it is true in the sense he sought, of models that could be built, cannot yet be answered."

**The unresolved fork:** Craik's thesis splits into (a) *symbolic-models-in-the-abstract* (true in some sense) and (b) *buildable physical models* (open question in 1951 — and the founding question of the computational theory of mind).

## 3. Connection to the Scansion Transform

The EEG section supplies the physiological substrate for Section 4.4's recognition mechanism: alpha rhythm as the scanning sweep that converts spatial arrays into temporal pulse trains. The EEG is Level 1 observation (measured voltage traces); the scansion transform is Level 2 algebraic structure; the recognition claim is Level 3 (Pitts-McCulloch).

## 4. Platform Coordinates

- **Confirmation hierarchy:** Wisdom distinguishes *information about* a mechanism from *support for* a hypothesis about that mechanism — a Level 4 epistemic move that the platform's basis-tier system makes explicit.
- **Craik as open node:** Under the OWA, Wisdom's "cannot yet be answered" is a live citation target: the buildability question attaches here and remains open for subsequent research programs.
