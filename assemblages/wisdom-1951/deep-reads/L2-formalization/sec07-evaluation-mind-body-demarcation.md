# Section 7: Evaluation of the Cybernetic Model & Mind-Body Demarcation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), pp. 22–24]*

## 1. Non-Newtonian Machines: Progress without Ontological Collapse

Wisdom addresses the recurring claim that cybernetics "proves" humans are machines:
- **The Epistemic Reversal:** Cybernetics does not show humans are clockwork; rather, it exposes why the classical Newtonian machine (levers, gears, deterministic trajectories) was hopelessly inadequate as an organic model.
- **The New Analogical Class:** Radar, servomechanisms, and thermostats are vastly superior analogies to clockwork, yet Wisdom cautions against dogmatic reification:
  > *"Brains are less like levers and gears than like radar and thermostats; the resemblance even to these is not very close, but it will have to serve until we find something better."*

## 2. Level 4 Demarcation: The Fallacy of the Mind-Body "Coup de Grâce"

Wisdom establishes a strict philosophical demarcation regarding the mind-body problem:
1. **The Incomplete Register:** Cybernetics models effector trajectories, error-correcting neural arcs, and behavioral adaptation.
2. **The Omission of Subjectivity:** It completely omits phenomenal consciousness, subjective experience, and intentional mental states.
3. **The Logical Fallacy:** Omitting the mental party from the equation does not constitute solving or dissolving the relation between mind and body:
   > *"It does not solve the problem of the relation of mind and body, because it omits one of the parties concerned and suffers from the one-sidedness of its ancestors."*
4. **Disciplinary Placement:** Cybernetics sits firmly within the historical lineage of physical physiology and clinical neurology. To proclaim it as a unified philosophy of mind commits an illicit bridge inference.

## 3. Machine-Addressable Grounding

- **Sign:** `cybernetic-mind-body-demarcation`
- **Sense:** The epistemic boundary establishing that while cybernetics successfully accounts for target-directed physiological regulation, it remains an extension of mechanistic physiology and cannot claim to have resolved phenomenal consciousness.
- **Nominatum:** Level 4 epistemic boundary integrity preserving the non-commutation of physical mechanism and phenomenal experience.
