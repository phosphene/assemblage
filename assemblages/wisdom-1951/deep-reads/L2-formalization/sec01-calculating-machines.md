# Section 1: Introduction. Automatic Calculating Machines

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), pp. 1–2]*

## 1. The Four-Functional Decomposition

Wisdom opens not with feedback but with the wartime electronic calculating machine, decomposing its capacities into four operations:

1. **(a) Intake:** to be fed with information
2. **(b) Storage:** to store results of preliminary calculations
3. **(c) Selection:** to select from its 'files' and use results appropriately at the right time
4. **(d) Output:** to report final results

He then maps these onto the intellect: *working out, storing, recalling, selecting, and communicating*.

## 2. The Chess Machine as Bounded Analogy

Wisdom's strategic move is to *undercut* the obvious analogy before it hardens into doctrine:

> "Indeed it is possible to design a machine to play chess without breaking the rules and even to make 'sound' moves, once it has been provided with some criterion of evaluation of moves. If this were all, however, the analogy between the machine and the brain or mind (we need not here be precise which) would hardly be striking."

**The carve:** rule-following computation with an evaluation criterion is *not* the philosophically interesting property. Chess-playing machines (Shannon 1950, referenced via the period's milieu) establish only that mechanical compliance with formal rules is possible. What is striking — and what motivates the whole paper — is the *special type of mechanism* these machines embody: feedback.

## 3. Significance for the Hierarchy

- **Constitutive claim:** Machine/brain analogy of the *functional* kind (a–d) is necessary but insufficient.
- **Causal mechanism:** The distinctively cybernetic property is the feedback mechanism (deferred to Section 2), not rule-execution.
- Wisdom's ordering is deliberate: he neutralizes the "machines think" reductionist reading *before* introducing the hypothesis, so that the strong claim lands on mechanism rather than on mimicry.

## 4. Platform Coordinates

- **Frege-Tellman sense:** The distinction between *rule-following computation* and *self-correcting mechanism* is the sense that organizes the entire paper.
- **Level 2 (epistemic-levels):** Wisdom here performs the mapping discipline the platform requires — noting the analogy, marking its limits, refusing to promote it prematurely.
