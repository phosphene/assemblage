# Section 2: Feedback & Amplification Taxonomy

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), pp. 3–6]*

## 1. Definitional Foundations

Wisdom defines feedback by explicitly decomposing the relationship between driving input energy and regulatory output control:

$$\text{Simple Negative Feedback} \iff \text{Part of input energy is redirected at intervals to check output energy}$$

### The Four-Quadrant Taxonomy

```
                     Negative (Damping/Corrective)        Positive (Accelerating/Reinforcing)
                  ┌─────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────┐
  Simple          │ Governor, Escapement,           │ Unclogging mechanism,           │
  (Derivative)    │ Thermostat (Energy redirected)  │ Chute bypass (Energy redirected)│
                  ├─────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────┤
  Amplification   │ Servo-mechanism, Power-steering │ Chain reaction, Nuclear fission,│
  (Tapped Local)  │ Controlled hydraulic damping    │ Wage-price spiral, Animosity    │
                  └─────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Constitutive vs. Energetic Distinction

1. **Simple Feedback:**
   - In Watt's centrifugal governor, clock escapements, and gas incubator balloons, the regulatory action expends *only* derivative energy tapped from the prime mover.
   - The feedback loop cannot introduce new energetic capacity into the system; its gain is strictly bounded by the primary drive.

2. **Amplification (Tapped) Feedback:**
   - The error-sensing component acts as a low-energy gate controlling an *auxiliary, independent reservoir of local energy*.
   - **Positive Amplification:** Demonstrates supercritical runaway dynamics. Wisdom explicitly groups physical (coal combustion, nuclear fission), economic (wage-price inflationary spirals), and sociopsychological phenomena (interpersonal animosity spirals).
   - This directly establishes the formal foundation later adopted by Gregory Bateson in his theory of *symmetrical and complementary schismogenesis*.

## 3. Machine-Addressable Grounding

- **Sign:** `amplification-feedback`
- **Sense:** A circular regulatory loop whose control signal modulates an independent, high-capacity local energetic reservoir rather than passively redirecting primary driving energy.
- **Nominatum:** High-gain servo-mechanisms and exponential runaway spirals ($dy/dt = k \cdot y$).
