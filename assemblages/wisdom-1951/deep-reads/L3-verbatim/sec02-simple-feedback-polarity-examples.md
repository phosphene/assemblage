# L3 Verbatim — §2.0–2.1: Simple Feed-Back, Polarity, and the Hovering Criterion

## Witness 1 — The Negative Polarity Definition (§2.1, p. 3)

> *"These are all examples of 'negative feed-back'. In all of them the work done by a source of power is controlled—the machine is prevented from running away—by a mechanism that checks it and keeps the rate at which energy is expended hovering round a certain quantity. This feed-back is 'negative' because the work done by the feed-back mechanism opposes the working of the main driving force."*
> *(Section 2.1, p. 3 [PDF p. 4])*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **Frege-Tellman Sense:** The sign `negative` is fixed by the **opposition criterion** — the feedback work vector *opposes* the driving force. Polarity is an energetic relation, not a value judgment.
- **The Hovering Criterion:** "hovering round a certain quantity" is the operational signature of regulated steady state — continuous small error with continuous correction. Wisdom refuses the idealization of constancy; the regulated quantity is the *mean of oscillations*, never the oscillation-free value.
- **Constitutive vs. Causal:** What feedback *is* — a topology where part of the output-energy re-enters as input; what it *does* — keeps the expenditure rate hovering. These are separated here and must not be conflated in downstream formalization.
- **Platform resonance (`concepts/epistemic-levels` Level 2):** This is the algebraic kernel that Shannon's channel theory and servo theory share without being identical — the isomorphism is structural, not physical.

## Witness 2 — The Positive Polarity and the Graduated-Start Rationale (§2.1, p. 3)

> *"Here, not only does the incubator become hotter simply through the consumption of a steady stream of gas, but a 'positive' feed-back mechanism increases the flow of the gas. This may seem to be a futile type of mechanism—for why not remove the balloon, open the tap, and have the maximum flow of gas from the outset? The reason is that there are circumstances when it is desirable to build up some required state at a slow pace. Suppose, for instance, we wish to run an electric train without a driver; we must not turn on full power at once; a positive feed-back must therefore be used to keep turning on more power gradually."*
> *(Section 2.1, p. 3 [PDF p. 4])*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **The Futility Objection and its Answer:** Wisdom anticipates the reader's charge that positive feedback is a pointless Rube Goldberg construction. His answer is a *temporal-shaping* function: positive feedback is the mechanism for imposing a controlled ramp on state-building. Full-power-at-once is available but undesirable; the loop's value is the *pace profile*, not the endpoint.
- **Platform resonance (Bateson schismogenesis):** This witness is the mechanical substrate beneath `work/research-wiki/wiki/biol/bateson-gregory.md` — symmetrical escalation (positive polarity) and its damping (negative polarity) are the same energetic topology applied to social regard and animosity (§2.2, p. 4, witness deposited in `sec02-amplification-feedback.md`).
- **Boundary condition:** Positive simple feedback is bounded by the driving source only — it cannot tap a local reservoir. That escalation-limit is what demarcates this witness from the amplification witness; when the loop outgrows the main source, it crosses into §2.2 territory.

## Witness 3 — The Driver-Source Exclusivity Clause (§2.1–2.2 boundary, p. 4)

> *"In all these mechanisms the energy of the feed-back comes from the given source of power driving the machine; the negative feed-back mechanisms—governors, escapements, thermostats—are simply devices for re-directing some of the energy supplied by the source of power against the rest of that energy, and the positive feed-back mechanisms redirect some of the energy towards increasing the energy supplied by the source of power—towards removing a blockage in fact. Both negative and positive types here described may be referred to as 'simple feed-back mechanisms'."*
> *(Section 2.1–2.2, p. 4 [PDF p. 5])*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **Taxonomic Anchor:** This paragraph is the *definitional closure* of the simple-feedback class. The criterion is purely energetic-provenance: if feedback energy is derivative of the main source, the mechanism is simple — regardless of polarity. Amplification (§2.2) is introduced *only* as the violation of this exclusivity clause.
- **Frege-Tellman Nominatum:** In the eventual formalization, "simple" maps to a closed energy budget: the feedback gain channel draws from the same reservoir as the drive channel. "Amplification" maps to an open two-reservoir system.
- **Editorial note:** "towards removing a blockage in fact" is Wisdom's compressed description of positive feedback as unblocking — the runaway is not mere growth but the clearing of an obstacle to the main flow. This phrase is easy to overlook and is the semantic bridge to the inflationary-spiral and chain-reaction examples of §2.2.
