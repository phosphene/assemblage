# L3 Verbatim — §4.4: Recognition, Centering, and Scansion

## Witness 1 — The Superior Colliculus as Feedback Servo (§4.4, p. 16)

> *"McCulloch's hypothesis is that the superior colliculus computes this, (i.e. presumably is fitted with negative feed-backs whose function is equivalent to the pencil-and-paper calculation); the superior colliculus signals the resulting position, in the form of impulses proportional to the two co-ordinates, to the lateral and vertical eye-muscles; and the effect of these impulses on these muscles is to turn the visual axis from its fixation point to the centre of brightness. This continues until the fixation point coincides with the centre of brightness. Thus we have a hypothetical radar-like negative feed-back by which the focus of attention gravitates towards the centre of brightness."*
> *(Section 4.4, p. 16 [PDF p. 17])*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **Anatomy of the Claim:** The superior colliculus is hypothesized as a *computing servo*: it solves (functionally) the centre-of-brightness coordinate problem, emits impulse-coded position commands, and closes the loop through the eye muscles. This is the first concrete neuroanatomical localization of a feedback control loop in the paper — every earlier example (governor, thermostat, homeostat) was artificial.
- **Frege-Tellman Nominatum:** The referent is a two-variable minimization: the visual-axis error vector (fixation point − brightness centroid) driven to zero. "Presumably is fitted with negative feed-backs whose function is equivalent to the pencil-and-paper calculation" — Wisdom flags the functional-equivalence move explicitly; the claim is *computational role*, not anatomical mechanism.
- **Historical note:** McCulloch's original is "A Hypothesis Concerning Visual Perception" / the scansion papers of 1945–1947; the quote Wisdom uses next is verbatim McCulloch, held to second-order witness status.

## Witness 2 — The Recognition Gap: Wisdom's Decisive Objection (§4.4, p. 16)

> *"McCulloch then concludes: 'If a square should appear anywhere in the field, the eyes turn until it is centered, and what they see is the same, whatever the initial position of the square.' But unfortunately he does not explain how centering a square of brightness is equivalent to 'recognition' of squareness."*
> *(Section 4.4, p. 16 [PDF p. 17]; inner quote verbatim McCulloch)*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **The Gap, Precisely:** Centering delivers *invariance* — the retinal state is the same whatever the square's initial position. But invariance is not yet *recognition*: nothing in the servo loop asserts "this is a square." Wisdom's "unfortunately" is doing philosophical work: it names the difference between **registration** (state normalization) and **categorization** (pattern identity).
- **Platform resonance (`concepts/epistemic-levels` Level 3):** This is a first-order research-program critique executed with exactly the right force: McCulloch's program is evaluated on its local empirical terms, and found to cover invariance but not classification. The gap anticipates the modern distinction between *normalization* and *object recognition* — and the entire line of work (Pitts-McCulloch's 1947 group-invariance paper) that Wisdom reports next is the attempt to close it.
- **Second-order witness flag:** The McCulloch sentence is quoted by Wisdom; direct citation of McCulloch 1947 requires verification against the original before any downstream use.

## Witness 3 — The Scansion Hypothesis: Space into Time (§4.4, pp. 16–17)

> *"Let us now consider the scansion hypothesis. This idea, which was first suggested by Craik, is that the scansion mechanism in television might play a rôle in perception. The first thing to be done is to translate impulses stimulated by visual forms in space into impulses that are successive. The idea is conveyed readily by the metaphor of morse code, by which a visual 'V' is replaced by four impulses along a time-scale: short-short-short-long."*
> *(Section 4.4, pp. 16–17 [PDF pp. 17–18])*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **The Dimensional Reduction:** Scansion is the transformation of a two-dimensional spatial intensity field into a one-dimensional temporal impulse train. The morse-code 'V' (short-short-short-long) is the *compressed specification* of the entire mechanism — the visual form is re-expressed as a rhythm.
- **Why this matters to the platform:** The L0 compass registers "scansion transform" as one of the paper's technical contributions. This witness is its textual anchor. The subsequent passage (§4.4 continuation, p. 17) specifies the mechanism: the CRT electron jet sweeping the screen line-by-line, 25–50 frames per second, photon-electron interaction producing impulses. The full CRT description is Level 1 observational material and is summarized in L2 rather than witnessed here.
- **Link to §6:** The scansion hypothesis is what the EEG section (witness in `sec06-eeg-rhythms-and-cortical-scansion.md`) then tests physiologically — the alpha-rhythm as the brain's scanning jet. The two witnesses must be read as hypothesis (§4.4) and physiological projection (§6).

## Witness 4 — The Regenerative Loop: Form Without a Moment (§4.4, p. 17)

> *"In the second place, these impulses must be conceived as travelling round a regenerative loop; for, as McCulloch puts it, 'a train of impulses in a regenerative loop preserves the form of the fact without reference to the one particular moment when it was experienced.'"*
> *(Section 4.4, p. 17 [PDF p. 18]; inner quote verbatim McCulloch)*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **Memory as Circulation:** The regenerative loop is the paper's answer to the storage problem: a fact is not held in a place but *re-instantiated* by continuous circulation. "Preserves the form of the fact without reference to the one particular moment when it was experienced" — the form is invariant; the instant is irrelevant. This is Lorente de Nó's reverberating circuit (§4.3) recruited for the recognition problem.
- **Frege-Tellman Sense:** Note the exact wording: "the form of the fact," not "the fact." The loop stores a *pattern of temporal structure*, not a proposition. This anticipates, in 1951, the modern view of working memory as sustained activity rather than persistent inscription.
- **Platform resonance (`sci.bio/hickey-rich` values-over-places):** "Without reference to the one particular moment when it was experienced" is the temporal generalization of the value/place distinction — the loop's identity is the recurring pattern, not any token instantiation of it. Recorded in L4 lineages: Lorente de Nó → McCulloch-Pitts regenerative loops → this witness → the modern recurrent-network view.
- **Second-order witness flag:** McCulloch's inner quote requires verification against the original 1945–1947 scansion papers before downstream citation.
