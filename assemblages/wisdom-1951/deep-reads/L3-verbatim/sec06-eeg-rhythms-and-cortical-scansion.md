# L3 Verbatim: Electro-encephalography & Cortical Scansion Substrates

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), pp. 21–22; exact PDF text witnesses from JSTOR stable:685605]*

---

## 1. Primary Textual Witnesses

### Block 6.1: Photic Entrainment and Epileptic Synchronization (p. 21)

> "Thus an epileptic patient, whose rhythm in a resting state was first obtained, was confronted with a light flashing at regular intervals; when the frequency of the flashes became equal to or a multiple of his rhythm when resting, his new rhythm showed the characteristic form associated with an epileptic attack. 'This observation appears to support the hypothesis, based on study of analysed epileptic records, that certain types of seizure are due to exact synchronization of cerebral rhythms previously slightly out of step.'"
> 
> *(Footnote 1: W. G. Walter, V. J. Dovey, and H. Shipton, "Analysis of the Electrical Response of the Human Cortex to Photic Stimulation", Nature, London, 1946, 158, 541.)*

### Block 6.2: The Alpha-Rhythm as the Physical Substrate of the Scansion Transform (p. 21)

> "Different characteristic rhythms are labelled by Greek letters. Here we confine ourselves to the alpha-rhythm. This is recorded when a subject is relaxed with his eyes closed, and the rhythm alters when the eyes are opened. In general there is nothing surprising in finding such rhythms; for from electrodynamics we should expect to find waves associated with electric circuits. What the precise connections between these waves and the various electroneural circuits are is unknown.
> 
> But one particular link is widely assumed, and this relates EEG to cybernetics: it is conjectured that the alpha-rhythm is associated with the scansion mechanism."

## 3. Analytical Commentary & Epistemic Decomposition

### A. Phase Entrainment as Non-Linear Coupling
In Block 6.1, Wisdom incorporates W. Grey Walter’s seminal 1946 *Nature* experiments:
- Photic driving: pulsing stroboscopic light at specific harmonics entrains cortical electrodynamics into hypersynchronous discharge.
- This is the classic signature of coupled non-linear oscillators undergoing frequency-locking and phase-synchronization (anticipating Winfree, Kuramoto, and modern dynamical neuroscience).
- Wisdom recognizes this as mechanical evidence that the brain's macroscopic rhythms are not inert epiphenomena, but functional coordinate systems that can be driven into resonance catastrophes.

### B. The Alpha-Rhythm as Biological Clock / Raster Generator
In Block 6.2, Wisdom grounds Pitts and McCulloch’s visual scanning transform in empirical neurophysiology:
- In Section 4.4, Pitts and McCulloch postulated a mathematical scanning mechanism to convert two-dimensional spatial geometries into one-dimensional temporal trains of invariant pitch.
- Where is the biological clock or raster beam in the cortex? Early cyberneticists conjectured that the **alpha-rhythm (8–13 Hz)** provides the sweeping carrier wave.
- When the eyes open, alpha-desynchronization occurs (the rhythm blocks or shifts to high-frequency beta activity) precisely because the resting sweep is now modulated by active visual error signals.
