# L3 Verbatim: The Mind-Body Demarcation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5), p. 23]*

## 1. The Demarcation Passage (verbatim)

> "Now it is true that cybernetics leaves out the mental aspect of action. But the proper inference from this is that cybernetics is in the tradition of physiology and neurology. It does not solve the problem of the relation of mind and body, because it omits one of the parties concerned and suffers from the one-sidedness of its ancestors."

## 2. Why This Passage Matters

1. **Inference discipline:** Wisdom does not conclude that cybernetics *fails* because it omits mind; he concludes that the correct inference is *scope limitation*. The failure mode he is correcting is the popular press's: claiming cybernetics "explains the mind" when it models the machinery of behavior.
2. **Genealogical diagnosis:** "Suffers from the one-sidedness of its ancestors" — cybernetics inherits the blind spots of physiology and neurology (Wiener's own physiological framing, McCulloch's neuronal epistemology). The omission is not accidental but *hereditary*.
3. **Level 4 boundary integrity (platform coordinates):** On the Four-Level Stack (`concepts/epistemic-levels`), Wisdom is enforcing the boundary between Level 3 mechanistic programs (legitimate) and Level 4 bridge claims (illicit here). Cybernetics models the circular effector arcs of behavior — it does not and cannot, within its own resources, close the bridge to phenomenal consciousness.
4. **Anticipates second-order cybernetics:** The omission Wisdom identifies in 1951 is precisely the gap von Foerster's "cybernetics of cybernetics" (1974) and Maturana-Varela's autopoiesis address: the observer was excluded from the observed system. Wisdom's critique is thus the *pre-registered* statement of the problem second-order cybernetics was invented to solve.

## 3. The Structural Complement

Wisdom's demarcation has two faces:

- **What cybernetics covers:** purpose, adaptation, prediction, regulation, recognition, memory — all as *behavioral machinery* (Levels 1–3).
- **What it omits:** "the mental aspect of action" — subjective experience, the observer's own phenomenal field.

The platform's reading: this is not a defect but a *demarcation* — the same discipline the platform applies to any single-research-program claim. A program that models one party to a two-party problem (mind and body) cannot, by its own resources, solve the relation. It can only misrepresent itself by claiming to.

## 4. Technical Vocabulary Introduced

- **One-sidedness:** hereditary scope limitation of a research program inherited from its ancestral disciplines.
- **Proper inference:** the epistemically licensed move from "X omits Y" to "X is a theory of scope-A" (not "X refutes Y" and not "X explains Y").
