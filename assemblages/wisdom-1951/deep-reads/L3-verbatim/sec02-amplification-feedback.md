# L3 Verbatim + Commentary — Section 2.2: Amplification Feed-Back and Energetic Tapping

## Primary Textual Witness (Wisdom 1951, pp. 4–5 [PDF pp. 5–6]) — COMPLETE, zero ellipses

> "2.2 Amplification Feed-Back. In none of the foregoing examples does a local source of energy arising from the feed-back mechanism play a part. The energy of the feed-back is derivative and redirected. But an additional source of energy may be exploited and utilised in the same sort of way. For example, in the combustion of coal, the lighted paper ignites some coal which releases vastly greater energy which fires more coal and so on. Again, in the uranium bomb, a neutron splits an atomic nucleus which releases energy and more neutrons which split more atomic nuclei and release more energy. Any chain-reaction mechanism is an illustration. Another example is the inflationary spiral of increased wages leading to increased prices leading to increased wages. If we consider two human beings, the regard or animosity of one for the other may build up through positive feed-back—or it may become damped or allayed by negative feed-back. The additional source of energy may conveniently be spoken of as "local", as contrasted with the main or central source. Here again we have negative or positive feed-back according to whether the locally tapped energy is fed back to impede or facilitate the main motion of the machine. When the feed-back is due in any measure to the exploitation of local energy we may speak of "amplification". The energy of a feed-back may be solely derived from the main power or solely due to a local source, or to both, and it may be desirable to distinguish the regulating energy and mechanism as "simple feed-back", "amplification feed-back", and "mixed feed-back". These are not well-distinguished in the literature, but in the field of cybernetics it is simple feed-back—and mainly negative at that—that writers usually have in mind. But this restriction is not required, and in what follows "feed-back" will be used indifferently for any form of the phenomenon; where determinateness is needed the appropriate qualifying word will be added."

> **COMPLETENESS CORRECTION (2026-11-26):** The witness previously deposited here was truncated — it ended at "damped or allayed by negative feed-back." and omitted the entire remainder of §2.2, which contains (a) the local/central source terminology, (b) the explicit negative-amplification clause, (c) Wisdom's actual **three-way** energetic taxonomy (simple / amplification / **mixed**), and (d) the terminological restriction note. The full passage above is extracted verbatim from the JSTOR scan (PDF pp. 5–6) with zero ellipses.

## Technical Context & Analytical Commentary

### 1. Why This Passage Matters
In 1951, early cybernetic discussions routinely conflated the informational topology of a loop with its energetic realization. Wisdom identifies the critical engineering and biological distinction:
- Most classical regulators (governors, escapements) are strictly *parasitic* on the kinetic or mechanical energy of the primary drive.
- When a feedback mechanism gates or triggers an **auxiliary, independent local energy reservoir**, the dynamics transform fundamentally. It gains the capacity for explosive non-linear amplification and positive runaway spirals.
- **The polarity clause for amplification is explicit:** "Here again we have negative or positive feed-back according to whether the locally tapped energy is fed back to impede or facilitate the main motion of the machine." Negative amplification = locally tapped energy fed back to *impede*; positive amplification = locally tapped energy fed back to *facilitate*.
- **Wisdom's taxonomy is three-way, not two-way:** "The energy of a feed-back may be solely derived from the main power or solely due to a local source, or to both, and it may be desirable to distinguish the regulating energy and mechanism as 'simple feed-back', 'amplification feed-back', and 'mixed feed-back'." The **mixed** class (feedback energy drawn from both main and local sources) is absent from the L2 2×2 taxonomy and must be added as a third energetic class.
- **Literature diagnosis:** Wisdom notes "in the field of cybernetics it is simple feed-back—and mainly negative at that—that writers usually have in mind. But this restriction is not required." §7 (p. 22) reinforces this: "The possibility should also be explored of finding amplification feed-backs, both positive and negative, in which local sources of energy are tapped."

### 2. Conceptual & Historical Lineages
- **Gregory Bateson & Schismogenesis:** This passage anticipates Gregory Bateson's (*Naven*, 1936; *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*, 1972) formalization of *symmetrical schismogenesis* (rivalry fueling rivalry) and *complementary schismogenesis* (dominance breeding submissiveness) as positive amplification loops in interpersonal and cultural anthropology.
- **Pathological Physiology:** Wisdom cites J. F. Thompson's clinical observation of renal hypertension: renal ischemia triggers renin release, elevating blood pressure, inducing further vascular kidney damage, and driving a lethal positive amplification spiral.
