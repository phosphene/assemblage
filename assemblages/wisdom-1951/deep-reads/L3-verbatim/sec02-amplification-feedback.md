# L3 Verbatim + Commentary — Section 2.2: Amplification Feed-Back and Energetic Tapping

## Primary Textual Witness (Wisdom 1951, p. 5 [PDF p. 6])

> "2.2 Amplification Feed-Back. In none of the foregoing examples does a local source of energy arising from the feed-back mechanism play a part. The energy of the feed-back is derivative and redirected. But an additional source of energy may be exploited and utilised in the same sort of way. For example, in the combustion of coal, the lighted paper ignites some coal which releases vastly greater energy which fires more coal and so on. Again, in the uranium bomb, a neutron splits an atomic nucleus which releases energy and more neutrons which split more atomic nuclei and release more energy. Any chain-reaction mechanism is an illustration. Another example is the inflationary spiral of increased wages leading to increased prices leading to increased wages. If we consider two human beings, the regard or animosity of one for the other may build up through positive feed-back—or it may become damped or allayed by negative feed-back."

## Technical Context & Analytical Commentary

### 1. Why This Passage Matters
In 1951, early cybernetic discussions routinely conflated the informational topology of a loop with its energetic realization. Wisdom identifies the critical engineering and biological distinction:
- Most classical regulators (governors, escapements) are strictly *parasitic* on the kinetic or mechanical energy of the primary drive.
- When a feedback mechanism gates or triggers an **auxiliary, independent local energy reservoir**, the dynamics transform fundamentally. It gains the capacity for explosive non-linear amplification and positive runaway spirals.

### 2. Conceptual & Historical Lineages
- **Gregory Bateson & Schismogenesis:** This passage anticipates Gregory Bateson's (*Naven*, 1936; *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*, 1972) formalization of *symmetrical schismogenesis* (rivalry fueling rivalry) and *complementary schismogenesis* (dominance breeding submissiveness) as positive amplification loops in interpersonal and cultural anthropology.
- **Pathological Physiology:** Wisdom cites J. F. Thompson's clinical observation of renal hypertension: renal ischemia triggers renin release, elevating blood pressure, inducing further vascular kidney damage, and driving a lethal positive amplification spiral.
