# L3 Verbatim — §7: The Non-Newtonian Machine

## Witness 1 — The Scope Correction: Not "Are Machines?" but "What Kind?" (§7, p. 22)

> *"Cybernetics does not, of course, show that human beings are machines. Many thinkers would hold that they are; but the important question concerns the kind of machine they are likely to be. When Ashby shows that an organism has certain properties of a machine, this does not establish that they have only those properties. And it is obvious to anyone who has ever met a dog or a man that organisms are vastly different machines from electronic calculators. What cybernetics does is to show us at least one new kind of machine. One way of putting this is to say that they are not machines in the Newtonian sense. Nor, however, are they machines in the Maxwell or in the Einstein sense."*
> *(Section 7, p. 22 [PDF p. 23])*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **The Demarcation of the Claim:** This is the paper's exactness discipline at its sharpest. The machine-thesis is decomposed: (i) organisms *have* machine properties (Ashby's demonstration — accepted); (ii) having some machine properties does not entail having *only* machine properties (the scope restriction); (iii) the real question is *classification*: which machine-kinds are organisms?
- **The Dry Homiletic Stroke:** "obvious to anyone who has ever met a dog or a man" — Wisdom deploys common experience as an epistemic checkpoint against overgeneralization from the model to the modeled. The phrase is a caution for the entire research program: the homeostat's success proves the *viability of the machine-kind*, not the *identity of the organism*.
- **Frege-Tellman Sense:** The sign `machine` is here given a *genus* structure — Newtonian, Maxwellian, Einsteinian, cybernetic — each a determinate machine-kind. Ashby's §4.0 definition (each configuration determines the following; witness in `sec04-steady-motion-equilibrium-types.md`) is the *genus* definition; the feedback mechanism is the *differentia* of the cybernetic species.

## Witness 2 — The Superset Relation: Feedback Goes Beyond Newton Without Violating Him (§7, p. 22)

> *"A Newtonian machine, for example, is one for which we can calculate velocities and positions resulting from given velocities and positions. Now a cybernetic machine does not violate the principles of such a machine; it is simply that feed-back mechanisms go beyond them. From the time of Newton or Laplace the idea was in the air that human beings were machines; yet this was obviously incongruous. Cybernetics shows why: the Newtonian machine was not an adequate model of a human being. The question now arises whether the..."*
> *(Section 7, p. 22 [PDF p. 23]; sentence continues p. 23, see sec07-mind-body-physiological-ancestry.md)*

### Annotation

*[read-together — Wisdom 1951 BJPS 2(5)]*

- **The Superset Claim, Stated Exactly:** "does not violate the principles of such a machine; it is simply that feed-back mechanisms go beyond them." Cybernetic machines are *consistent with* Newtonian mechanics and *incomplete under* Newtonian description. The feedback relation is not anti-Newtonian physics; it is a higher-order organizational description that Newtonian machinery cannot express — circular causality, error-actuated regulation, and (in Ashby's case) step-function reorganization do not appear as primitive terms in the Newtonian vocabulary.
- **The Laplacean Diagnosis:** "the idea was in the air that human beings were machines; yet this was obviously incongruous. Cybernetics shows why." This is the paper's deepest historical claim: the incongruity was never between mechanism and mind in general — it was between the *Newtonian machine-kind* and the phenomena. The Laplacean demon was not too mechanistic; it was *too narrowly* mechanistic.
- **Platform resonance (`concepts/epistemic-levels` Level 4):** This is the positive half of Wisdom's §7 evaluation — the bridge claim that *is* licensed: cybernetics extends the machine-genus with a new species. What remains unlicensed (Witness 1's restriction + the mind-body demarcation witness in `sec07-mind-body-physiological-ancestry.md`) is claiming the new species exhausts the organism.
- **Compression register (`concepts/specification-compression-relation`):** "feed-back mechanisms go beyond them" is the compressed specification of the entire §2–§4 argumentative arc: from Watt governor through ultrastability, every mechanism is Newtonian physics *plus* a closed causal loop that the Newtonian description does not and cannot pick out.
